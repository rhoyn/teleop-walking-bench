# Commanded vs measured movement

Generated by `build/cmd_vs_real_movements` from `results/{rotate,direction}_gainx*`.

Each run holds one command for 10 s: a yaw rate in `rotate`, a planar velocity in `direction`. Measured rates are taken over t = 2 s to the end, runs that fell excluded. A policy is charted when its legs KP/KD x1.0 completion averages > 95% over both engines and benchmarks, and a gain is drawn when rotate completes > 99% and direction > 95% at it.

Ranking uses the first accepted gain (x1.0, else x1.5, else x2.0): linear real vs commanded walk speed (direction) and rotation speed (rotate) are R^2 of the line fitted to measured vs commanded with both engines pooled, the share of the measured speed's variance the line explains, higher is better; the walk and rotation deadzones are the command needed to reach a measured 0.025, averaged over both engines (1.0 when never reached). Each of the four is ranked on its own, 1 = lowest, and policies are ordered by the sum of the four ranks.
