# Commanded vs measured movement

Generated by `build/cmd_vs_real_movements` from `results/{rotate,direction}_gainx*`.

Each run holds one command for 10 s: a yaw rate in `rotate`, a planar velocity in `direction`. Measured rates are taken over t = 2 s to the end, runs that fell excluded. A policy is charted when its legs KP/KD x1.0 completion averages > 95% over both engines and benchmarks, and a gain is drawn when rotate completes > 99% and direction > 95% at it.

Precision is scored on small commands, 0.05 to 0.15 m/s or rad/s, by movement along the command, both engines pooled, at the first accepted gain (x1.0, else x1.5, else x2.0). Error is the median distance between measured and commanded velocity relative to the command, walk and turn averaged (in rotate the planar drift counts as error): 0% is perfect, 100% is what standing still scores, above 100% the robot moves the wrong way. Right way is the share of runs moving at least 0.025 along the command; delivered is the median fraction of the command achieved along it; drift at zero is the median planar speed for direction commands under 0.02 m/s. Rows are ordered by error.
